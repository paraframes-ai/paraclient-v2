ParaFrames

An AI tutor that helps students learn — and keeps their data on-prem.

ParaFrames is an educational assistant for students. It guides you through problems with questions instead of just handing over answers, works across subjects, and runs its core tutoring on our own servers — so a student’s work stays private by design.

What ParaFrames does

A single tutor that meets students where they are — from a math walkthrough to a first circuit — while keeping a teacher’s judgment about how to help.

Socratic tutoring

Guides, doesn’t just answer

Across math, science, language arts, and civics, ParaFrames asks leading questions and escalates to hints and worked steps — so students build understanding, not just copy a result.

Make & build

Diagrams, sheets, circuits

Describe what you need and ParaFrames drafts a 2D/3D CAD sketch, a working spreadsheet, or a beginner circuit — each checked by a deterministic validator before you see it.

Knowledge Library

Your notes, searchable

Snap a photo of handwritten notes to turn them into editable text and math, and keep your materials in a personal library you can ask questions against.

Efficient by design

Capability, not just scale

ParaFrames runs efficient models on modest hardware — a research focus on getting frontier-quality help without frontier-scale cost or energy.

Built for students, safe by design

Every message is screened by on-device safety moderation before a student ever sees a reply, and ParaFrames is age-gated at sign-up: consumer accounts are for ages 13 and up, and under-13 students use ParaFrames only through their school. Student content is processed on our own servers and is never used to train our models or sold. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
