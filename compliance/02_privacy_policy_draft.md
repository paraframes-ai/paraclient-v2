## Who this covers

ParaFrames serves two groups:

- **Consumer users (age 13 and older).** You sign up directly.
- **Students (under 13) through their school.** Under-13 users access ParaFrames
  **only** through a school. For those users, the school directs our handling of
  student data and this policy is subject to our agreement with the school.

**We do not knowingly allow under-13 users to sign up directly.** Our sign-up asks
your age; anyone under 13 is directed to the school path and no account is created.
If we learn we have collected data from an under-13 user outside the school path,
we delete it (see "Children under 13").

## What we collect

- **Account info:** email, and a name if you provide one.
- **Age verification:** at consumer sign-up we confirm you are 13+ (we keep the
  verification result, not necessarily your full birth date).
- **Your content:** the messages you send the tutor, files you upload to your
  Knowledge Library, and images of notes you ask us to transcribe.
- **Safety signals:** automated content-safety screening of messages.
- **Technical logs:** access and rate-limiting logs.

See `03_data_map.md` for the full inventory.

## How we use it

- To provide the tutoring, generation, transcription, and Knowledge-Library
  features you request.
- To keep the service safe (automated content moderation, especially for minors).
- To operate, secure, and debug the service.

**We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use your
content to train our models** — model improvement uses separately-sourced,
license-clean datasets, not user content. `<FILL: confirm.>`

## Where your data goes (and where it doesn't)

ParaFrames runs on our own infrastructure. Your content is processed on our
on-premise servers.

- **Students (edu):** student content is processed **entirely on our own servers
  and is never sent to any third-party AI provider or external web service.**
- **Consumer users (13+):** if you use a paid feature powered by an external model
  (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude, provided via Google Cloud Vertex AI) or a
  live web-lookup feature, the necessary content is sent to that provider to
  fulfill your request. These features are opt-in and are **not available on the
  student/edu path.** Sub-processors: `<FILL: Google LLC (Vertex AI), Anthropic PBC>`.

## Children under 13

ParaFrames is **not** offered directly to children under 13. Under-13 use is only
through a participating school under a written agreement in which the school acts
as a "school official" and provides the required consent on parents' behalf. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 outside that
school relationship; if we learn we have, we delete it promptly. Parents at a
participating school should contact their school, or us at `<FILL>`.
